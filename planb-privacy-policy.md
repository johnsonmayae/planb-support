# Plan B: Second Best — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 20, 2026*

Plan B (Second Best) ("the App") is developed by Maya Johnson. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

The App does not require account creation or login. We do not collect your name, email, or personal information directly.

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and related usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

## How Information Is Used

Advertising data collected by AdMob is used to display relevant ads. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
