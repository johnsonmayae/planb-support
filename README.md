## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I use Plan B?**
After your opponent moves, tap the PLAN B button to cancel their move and force them to play differently. You can only use it once per game.

**What is No Mercy mode?**
In No Mercy mode, winning moves cannot be cancelled with Plan B.

**How do I play vs the computer?**
From the home screen, tap PLAY VS COMPUTER and choose a difficulty level.
